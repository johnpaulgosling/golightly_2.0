\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:disc}

We have separated two errors that a nested ensemble Kalman filter can make, and we have compared the three targets those errors induce on a nonlinear observation. The plain filter targets \(\hat{\pi}_{\mathrm{enkf}}\). A nearest-neighbour incremental weight targets \(\pi_{\mathrm{knn}}\). The Rao-Blackwellised weight puts the true observation density back and targets \(\tilde{\pi}_{\mathrm{enkf}}\), which is still not the exact posterior. Bootstrap SMC2 is the scheme, among the three we ran, that targets \(\pi_t\).

Lemma~\ref{lem:identity} is the linear Gaussian case of the Rao-Blackwellised weight. On the Ornstein--Uhlenbeck pilot the two log increments agreed exactly, so the correction is not worth its cost when the filter is already using the true observation density. The same pilot's surrogate diagnostic stayed inside the threshold fixed in advance: the mean of the median absolute log differences was 0.277, against a threshold of 1. A bound on the distance between \(\pi_{\mathrm{knn}}\) and \(\hat{\pi}_{\mathrm{enkf}}\) was not obtained. A uniform bound on the log-likelihood difference would control the total variation distance, and that hypothesis is not available for a likelihood that is redrawn with the ensemble.

On the Lotka--Volterra series the plain filter's means of \(\log\theta_2\) and \(\log\theta_3\) lie below the SMC2 means, by 0.0586 and 0.0354. The standard errors are 0.0865 and 0.0953, and the pre-registered rule therefore leaves the shift unresolved. The Rao-Blackwellised filter is closer to SMC2 on \(\log\theta_2\) and not on \(\log\theta_3\). \citet{golightly2026} report a downward shift for the plain filter, reduced by the Rao-Blackwellised weight, at \(M = 1000\) and against a long particle Markov chain Monte Carlo run. Our comparison uses \(M = 60\), four replicates and SMC2 itself as the reference, so it can show the shift and it can miss it. Here it does not resolve it. The result does not confirm that ordering, and it does not contradict it.

The limits of the comparison are the ones the design named. There is one series. The state-particle count in SMC2 is fixed at 24, below the terminal count in Section~5.2 of \citet{golightly2026}, and a particle Markov chain Monte Carlo gold standard was not run. The standard errors are estimated from four replicates. A small fraction of Euler draws met the positive floor. The Euler counts omit the cost of the density ratio in the Rao-Blackwellised weight. The Gamma priors use a shape-rate reading that the source paper does not state. These are reasons to treat the unresolved verdict as a statement about this run.

An exact neighbour tree, a kernel switch that depends only on time, and a finite-difference proposal were part of the earlier pilot. They are not recommended here. The finite-difference proposal leaves \(\hat{\pi}_{\mathrm{enkf}}\) invariant and did not improve effective sample size per transition draw. What remains open is a bound on \(\pi_{\mathrm{knn}}\) that can be checked, and a repeat of the Lotka--Volterra comparison at the particle counts of Section~5.2. We do not add an epidemic series. \citet{temfack2026} already study a nested ensemble scheme for incidence.
